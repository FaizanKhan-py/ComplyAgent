# Customer Contact Policy (Sample, fictional)

Policy ID: CP-01. Applies to all collections staff and to any automated agent acting on their behalf.

## 1. Permitted contact hours

Customers may only be contacted between 8:00 a.m. and 9:00 p.m. in the customer's local time zone. No contact of any kind is permitted on public holidays. If the customer's time zone is unknown, staff must assume the most restrictive time zone consistent with the customer's postal address.

## 2. Contact frequency

A customer must not receive more than three contact attempts in any seven-day period across all channels combined. A contact attempt is any phone call, voicemail, SMS, or email that concerns the overdue balance. After a live conversation with the customer, no further contact attempt may be made for at least 48 hours unless the customer asks for one.

## 3. Channels

Email and SMS may only be used when the customer has given consent to that channel and the consent is recorded in the customer record. Voicemail messages must not mention the debt amount. Staff must never contact a customer's employer, relatives, or neighbours about a debt, except to obtain contact details, and then only once.

## 4. Tone and content

Every message must state the name of the organisation, the reason for the contact, and how the customer can respond. Messages must not threaten legal action unless legal action has been approved under the Escalation Policy. Messages must not be sent to a customer who has an open dispute until the dispute is resolved.

## 5. Requests to stop contact

If a customer asks in writing for contact to stop, all outbound contact must cease within two business days. The account must then be referred to a team lead, who decides the next step.
