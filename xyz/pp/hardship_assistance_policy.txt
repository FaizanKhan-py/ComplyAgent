HARDSHIP ASSISTANCE POLICY (Sample, fictional)
Policy ID: HA-02

1. Purpose

This policy describes when a customer may be offered a hardship plan and what a hardship plan may contain. It applies to every loan account that is at least one day overdue.

2. Eligibility

A customer is eligible for a hardship plan when they report a loss of income, a medical emergency, a natural disaster, or the death of a household member, and the event happened within the last 12 months. Staff must record the reason given by the customer. Supporting documents must be requested, but a plan may start before they arrive. If documents are not received within 30 days, the plan is reviewed by a team lead.

3. What a plan may offer

A hardship plan may reduce the monthly installment by up to 50 percent for a maximum of six months. Late fees are waived for the duration of the plan. Interest continues to accrue at the contract rate. A customer may have no more than one hardship plan in any 24-month period.

4. Approval

Plans that reduce the installment by 25 percent or less may be approved by any collections officer. Plans that reduce the installment by more than 25 percent must be approved by a team lead. No automated agent may approve a hardship plan; an agent may only recommend one for human review.

5. Ending a plan

A plan ends early if the customer misses two consecutive reduced payments. When a plan ends, the account returns to the standard collections process and the customer must be told in writing within five business days.
